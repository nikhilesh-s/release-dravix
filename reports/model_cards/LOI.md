# Model card: LOI (limiting oxygen index, %)
Source of every number: `experiments/numbers_of_record.json` (exp092) = `experiments/results/exp092_loi_r5_c2_f6fixed_std_logdens.json` (round 5, candidate c2 "f6fixed_std_logdens" of `experiments/configs/loi_round5.json`, declared before it ran; critic `experiments/critic/loi_round5_critic.md`, SOUND WITH CAVEATS). No promoted joblib.

**Intended use.** Research baseline for LOI of rigid polyurethane foams (RPUF) from flame-retardant loading, class, element content, base-foam type and (for a few FRs) molecular structure. Not for design decisions.

**Data.** Independent literature reconstruction of the Sun/Fu RPUF population (not their 435 rows): `data/v2/loi_rpuf_literature.csv`, extraction != figure, loi_pct present: 460 rows / 83 source papers (waves 1-3; wave-3 QA 0 value errors). Hand extraction from papers; loadings normalised to foam wt% (`data/v2/build_loi_rpuf.py`); FR element table `fr_element_table.csv`. FR structures: `data/v2/fr_structures.csv` (PubChem/RDKit, resolved offline by `resolve_fr_structures.py`; 16 audited structures; 41/460 rows from 14 papers have structure features; training reads the CSV, no network).

**Features (51).** The exp089 set (45 = the exp087 set of 34 plus the round-4 additions) with the round-4 F6 base-foam block replaced by F6_fixed, plus two round-5 extras. exp087 set (34): the 27 round-1 features (total_fr_wtpct, 15 FR-class flags, foam-level element wt% P, N, Si, B, halogen, metal, density_kgm3, n_frs, has_EG, has_APP, reactive; element wt% recomputed with PubChem formulas for FRs missing from the element table), 6 RDKit FR-structure features (fr_arom_frac, fr_fsp3, fr_PO_per_kDa, fr_PN_per_kDa, fr_mollogp, fr_tpsa_per_kDa) and resolved_fr_wt_share. Kept from round 4: F7 class-prior fill of still-unresolved el_* (per-FR-class median element fractions over resolved FR mentions, no LOI used) plus el_imputed_share, F8 synergy (syn_PxN, syn_P_over_N, syn_EG_x_P, syn_totfr_x_dens). F6_fixed (7, replaces the 6 round-4 F6 columns): polyol_type one-hot with 5 levels (pt_polyether, pt_polyester_aromatic, pt_bio_based, pt_other_polyester, pt_unknown; polyol assigned by the component with the largest stated amount, polyether regex checked before bio), iso_index_num with the "R a:b" / "a:1" ratio parse, is_PIR not triggered by "isocyanurate-based polyether polyol". Round-5 extras (5): loi_standard one-hot (std_ASTM_D2863, std_GB_T_2406, std_ISO_4589, std_other_unknown; earliest regex match wins) and log_density.

**Model.** RandomForestRegressor(n_estimators=500, min_samples_leaf=2, random_state=0), NaN-native (exp085/exp087 config).

**Protocol.** Primary: GroupKFold by source paper 5x10; paper-bootstrap 95% CI.

**Number of record.** R2 0.3738, CI [0.2007, 0.4701] (results: rkf_mean 0.3738, ci_lo 0.2007, ci_hi 0.4701; std over repeats 0.0434), 460 rows / 83 papers. Pooled-repeat CI [0.2132, 0.4884]. Their random 80/20 protocol on our rows: 0.723 [0.579, 0.832] (gss_mean 0.7228). Held out by dominant FR class (protocol C): 0.256 (c_mean 0.2564; exp089 0.257). Paired vs exp089: +0.0196, 9/10 repeats, paper-bootstrap CI [-0.012, +0.059] (P(gain <= 0) = 0.12); stable across RF seeds 0-3 (+0.020 to +0.022, wins 8-9/10). Paired vs exp087: +0.063, 10/10 repeats, CI [+0.017, +0.133], optimistic after two rounds of max-of-candidates selection.

**History.** exp089 (round 4, train_loi_round4.py c2, same 460 rows / 83 papers): 0.354 [0.169, 0.466], their random split 0.700 (previous number of record). exp087 (460 rows / 83 papers): 0.311 [0.146, 0.467], their random split 0.658. exp085 (333 rows / 56 papers): 0.363 [0.167, 0.517]; exp064 on that snapshot 0.351. Earlier: exp041 FAA pure polymers 0.509 (wrong population).

**Known limitations** (record note and critic `experiments/critic/loi_round5_critic.md`)
- The paired gain over exp089 is +0.020 with a 95% paper-bootstrap CI [-0.012, +0.059] that includes 0: directional, not established. Do not call it a significant improvement. About 2/3 of it comes from the loi_standard one-hot, which was not on the round-4 critic's list; the typing fixes alone (c1) give +0.007 (CI [-0.012, +0.028]). The top 3 papers (N077, M079, M110) carry 82% of the SSE reduction; dropping any one leaves the gain at +0.013 to +0.016.
- loi_standard is paper-level test-method metadata (constant within every paper, so not leakage under GroupKFold by paper) but acts as a lab/region and missingness proxy: other_unknown is a missing standard (158 rows / 30 papers). It corrects a reporting-group offset, not chemistry, and only makes sense when predicting a LOI measured under a stated standard. log_density is a monotone transform of density_kgm3 and is redundant for an RF.
- The gain over exp087 (+0.063, CI [+0.017, +0.133]) comes from two rounds of max-of-candidates selection (3 candidates, then 2) after seeing results, so that CI is optimistic. A fair statement: about +0.04 to +0.06 over exp087 is probably real, the last +0.02 the least secure.
- Held out by FR class (protocol C) it stays at 0.256 (exp089 0.257; exp087 0.345): the class-prior fill helps within-class transfer and hurts cross-class transfer. Quote C next to B.
- Polyol typing is still noisy after the fix: glycolysed-PET strings (6 benchmark rows) and "polypropylene glycol (MCNS)" (9) are still unknown; new errors include "MCOP (castor) + PEG-200 (20 g)" typed polyether (6 rows), "liquefied pine sawdust biopolyol" typed polyether (PEG check runs first), and ESBO-DYM-majority rows (2) typed unknown. Left as declared; the extra-round budget for LOI is exhausted.
- B 0.374 (CI upper bound 0.470) is far below the Sun/Fu reference 0.84; their random-split protocol on our rows gives 0.723, still below. Never phrase this as beating it. Reconstruction, not the competitor's data: the gap mixes population, row count and protocol; the split alone moves R2 by ~0.35.
- FR-structure features cover only 41 of 460 rows (14 of 83 papers); the rest are NaN. Hand-extracted values; FRs without an element-table or PubChem entry get class-prior element values (el_imputed_share records how much). BH (a phosphonate) was resolved to bohrium and left unresolved.
- xgboost not installed, so no XGBoost comparison was run.
- F7 priors are computed on all rows, not per fold; they contain no LOI information (critic: acceptable).
